Common questions

Where are my thoughts stored?

On your device only, encrypted with AES-256. There is no account and no server, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do I move my thoughts to a new phone?

Export an encrypted backup from the old device, choose a password, save the file somewhere you can reach, then import it on the new device with the same password.

I forgot my export password. Can you recover it?

No. The password never leaves your device and the file cannot be decrypted without it. This is the trade-off that makes the encryption meaningful.

I deleted the app. Are my thoughts gone?

Yes. Deleting the app removes both your thoughts and the encryption key. Export a backup before deleting or resetting your device.

Is there a cloud sync?

Not today. Encrypted export and import is the way to move data between devices.

Can I read the code?

Yes. Thoughtbook is open source under GPLv3 at github.com/dev-shetty/thoughtbook.
