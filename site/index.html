Why Thoughtbook

Most thoughts never make it
out of your head

You either don't have someone to tell, or you're not comfortable sharing publicly. Over time, ideas fade, frustrations linger, and experiences go unrecorded.

Notion is overkill for capturing an instant. Twitter is distracting and public. The Notes app gets cluttered. You can't carry a physical journal everywhere. There was no middle ground for writing down a thought on the fly.

So I built one. Writing is one of the simplest ways to process what's on your mind. Thoughtbook gives you a space to do that — without worrying about who's reading.

Your thoughts are too precious to be lost in the void.

Features

Minimal by design, powerful by nature

End-to-end encrypted

256-bit encryption keys generated on your device, stored in the OS secure keychain. No plaintext ever leaves your phone.

Minimal interface

Open the app, write a thought, done. No accounts, no social features, no feeds. Just a blank page waiting for your words.

Offline-first

Everything runs and persists locally. No internet required, no servers involved. Works anywhere, anytime.

Favorites

Star the thoughts that matter most and find them instantly in a dedicated tab. Swipe to favorite from the feed.

Calendar heatmap

See your writing history at a glance. Color-coded days show how many thoughts you wrote, plus streak tracking to build a habit.

Tags

Categorize thoughts with #hashtags. Travel ideas, bucket lists, wishlists — find them later when you need them.

Security

Your data never leaves your device

Key generation

On first launch, a 256-bit random key is generated using the device's cryptographic API.

Secure storage

That key is stored in the OS secure keychain, accessible only when the device is unlocked.

Encrypted persistence

All thought data is encrypted at rest using MMKV storage. No plaintext is ever written to disk.

Biometric lock

Optionally protect the app with Face ID or fingerprint for an additional layer of security.
