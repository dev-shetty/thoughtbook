Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Thoughtbook does not collect, transmit, or store any of your journal content. Everything you write stays encrypted on your device. There is no account, no server, and no sync.

What the app stores

Your thoughts, their timestamps, and any tags you add are stored locally on your device. They are encrypted with AES-256, and the encryption key is generated on your device and held in the iOS Keychain (or the Android Keystore), never in the app's own storage.

Nobody but you can read this data. Not us, not Apple, and not anyone who gets hold of the raw files without your device's key.

What the app sends

Nothing you write. The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting that captures your content, and no tracking of any kind.

The app makes exactly one kind of network request:

  • Over-the-air updates. On launch, Thoughtbook checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) to see whether a newer version of the app code is available. This request includes technical information such as your app version, platform, and device identifiers required to serve the correct update. It never includes your journal content.

Export and backup files

When you export your thoughts, Thoughtbook produces an encrypted file protected by a password you choose, and hands it to the iOS or Android share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely your choice, whether that's Files, iCloud Drive, email, or another app, and the file's contents remain encrypted. We never receive it.

Keep your password. If you lose it, the export cannot be recovered by us or anyone else.

Deleting your data

Deleting a thought removes it from your device. Deleting the app removes all of your thoughts and the encryption key along with it. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, and no way for us to restore what is gone. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

This website

Page views are counted anonymously with Umami, which sets no cookies and does not track you across sites.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
